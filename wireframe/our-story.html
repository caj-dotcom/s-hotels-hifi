OUR STORY

S Hotels Jamaica History

Though officially named in 2024, the story of S Hotels Jamaica begins earlier, with Spanish Court Hotel in Kingston in 2009 and S Hotel Montego Bay in 2019, shaped by the belief that Jamaican art, music, and place belong inside the stay.

In 2009, Christopher Issa converted the Spanish Court Shopping Centre in Kingston into Spanish Court Hotel. What had been a commercial building became a calm urban hotel in New Kingston, close to the Bob Marley Museum and Devon House, with room for meetings, receptions, and guests who already knew the city.

A decade later, in 2019, the former Breezes property on Jimmy Cliff Boulevard was transformed into S Hotel Montego Bay. The building sits on the Hip Strip with direct access to Doctor’s Cave Beach. Life-size Jamaican icons were placed throughout the property, Bob Marley record players went into the rooms, and the Sky Deck and Irie Spa were added. It became a boutique all-inclusive sized for people who wanted the beach and the culture without the scale of a large resort.

In 2024 the two hotels were brought under one name. S Hotels Jamaica now holds both properties together under a single logo and a single website, while keeping their characters distinct. Kingston remains the city hotel. Montego Bay remains the beach hotel. What links them is the decision to treat Jamaican art, music, and place as part of the stay rather than as decoration.
