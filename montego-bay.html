Frequently Asked Questions

Is the hotel all-inclusive?

Yes. Meals and drinks are included across the restaurants and bars.

How close is Doctor’s Cave Beach?

The hotel has direct access to Doctor's Cave Beach.

Can I walk to the Hip Strip from the hotel?

Yes. The hotel is on Jimmy Cliff Boulevard, on the Hip Strip.

Are children allowed or children friendly ?

Children aged 8 and older are welcome. Some areas stay adult-only.

How far is the hotel from the airport?

About 5 minutes from Sangster International Airport.
