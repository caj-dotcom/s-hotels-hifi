JAMAICA IS SERVED

From Jamaican breakfast to a quiet lunch in the city

Dining at S Hotel Kingston is a calm part of the capital stay. The Jamaican breakfast is a regular highlight. Rocksteady handles business lunches and proper dinners. Strings is for Blue Mountain coffee, light plates, and an afternoon pause. The outlets stay simple and well looked after, built around the food and the people sitting down to it.

Rocksteady

Feast your senses on continental classics made with fresh local ingredients. Rocksteady restaurant’s modern, elegantly relaxed ambience is perfect for a romantic dinner or a business lunch.

Cabana Pool

Strings Cafe & Bar
