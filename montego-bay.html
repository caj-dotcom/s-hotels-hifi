Beach & atmosphere

Nobody is racing for a cabana.

Questions, answered here.

Is the hotel all-inclusive?

Yes. Meals and drinks are included. It is still a boutique hotel, not a large resort with a long activity sheet.

How close is Doctor's Cave Beach?

It is the beach at the hotel. Guests walk out to it.

Can I walk to the Hip Strip?

Yes. It is there when you want it, and the hotel stays quiet when you do not.

Are children welcome?

Children from 8 are welcome. Some areas of the hotel stay adult-only.

How far is the airport?

Sangster International is a short ride from the Hip Strip.
