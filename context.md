# S Hotels Jamaica — direction for the high-fidelity pages

This file is the brief. Follow it. Do not invent a different hotel, a different structure, or a luxury look that could be anywhere.

The current HTML in this repo is a first pass. The person reviewing it does not like the UI. Rebuild the visual design. Keep the page order.

## What this is

Three static pages, one brand, two hotels.

- `index.html` is the parent, S Hotels Jamaica.
- `montego-bay.html` is S Hotel Montego Bay.
- `kingston.html` is S Hotel Kingston.
- `styles.css` is shared.
- `parent.html` should stay the same page as `index.html`.

Phones and laptops, both. No sideways scrolling. Light motion only. Sharp corners. No rounded buttons, no rounded images.

## What premium means here

Not ultra luxury. Not marble, not thread counts, not a show.

Montego Bay premium is intimate and unhurried. A free cabana. A drink made without anyone watching the clock. Art on the way to the beach. Music at night. Staff known by name by day two. Casual, and well looked after.

Kingston is the same standard, but the city is the point. Home for diaspora and returning Jamaicans. Solid for business. Banqueting is a real business, not a footnote. It should feel premium and professional, never less polished than Montego Bay, and never like a beach resort.

Neither site should feel cheaper than the other.

## Look

- Warm, Jamaican, intentional. Not a generic resort template.
- Paper, ink, sand, deep green, brass. No red as a brand colour. A tiny red mark is allowed only if it almost disappears.
- Big serif headlines, plain sans for navigation and buttons.
- Full-width hero first. Booking bar directly under it. Then the story. Then social proof.
- Real photography energy: beach, food, art, rooms, the city. The Unsplash images in the files are stand-ins. Treat them as temporary. Do not use marble lobbies or stock that could be Dubai.
- When a hero can move, it should feel like a film of the hotel: pool, food, beach or city, massage. Still images are fine until real video exists.

## Who it is for

Montego Bay: couples, relaxed families with children 8 and up, solo travellers who want quiet, people celebrating something small.

Kingston: business guests, diaspora and returning Jamaicans, couples who want the city, people booking a meeting or a reception.

Do not design for club-goers, party tourists, or guests who expect a big resort with a long activity list. Do not hide the all-inclusive offer. Do not let it be the only story.

## How to work

Edit the HTML and CSS in this folder. Keep the three pages linked to each other. After a change, say what you changed in plain language. Do not add a framework.
