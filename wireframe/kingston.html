Frequently Asked Questions

What should I know about S Hotel Kingston before booking?

S Hotel Kingston is a boutique city hotel in New Kingston, strong on service, breakfast and events. It is not a beach resort.

Do you have meeting and banqueting facilities?

Yes. Banqueting is one of the hotel's strengths, with rooms for meetings, receptions and weddings.

Is the hotel suitable for business travellers?

Yes. Quiet rooms, free WiFi and wired internet, and the business district close by.

Are children allowed or children friendly ?

Families are welcome. Call the team to talk through the right room for your family.

Is the hotel close to the Bob Marley Museum?

Yes. The museum is a short trip from New Kingston, with Devon House nearby too.
