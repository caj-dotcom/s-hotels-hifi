CHARITY FOUNDATION

Help Jamaica rise again after hurricane Melissa

Hurricane Melissa has devastated Jamaica. Families have lost homes, livelihoods, and daily essentials.
The S Hotels Foundation is providing shelter, medical aid, food, and supplies to those most affected. S Hotels Jamaica will match every dollar donated, up to USD $100,000, so your gift goes twice as far.

Join us in rebuilding Jamaica. Every dollar helps.
