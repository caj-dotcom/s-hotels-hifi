Questions, answered here.

What should I know before booking?

This is a city boutique hotel. It is strong on service, breakfast, and events. It is not a beach resort.

Do you have meeting and banqueting facilities?

Yes. Banqueting is one of Kingston's strengths, for meetings, receptions, and weddings.

Is it suitable for business travellers?

Yes. That is a core guest. The stay should feel professional and still Jamaican.

Are children welcome?

Families do stay. The feeling of the hotel is calm and grown, suited to business and to people visiting home.

Is the hotel close to the Bob Marley Museum?

Yes. The museum is a short trip from New Kingston, and the hotel keeps art in its own common rooms.
