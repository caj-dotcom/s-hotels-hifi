Frequently Asked Questions

Something else on your mind? Talk to the team.

What is required to get married in Jamaica?

A marriage licence, valid ID and a short stay on the island. Our events team will walk you through each step.

What does it cost?

It depends on your guest count, venue and menu. Tell us about your plans and we will send a tailored quote.

What if I’m traveling from another country?

Many of our couples fly in. We can help with travel dates, paperwork timing and rooms for your guests.
